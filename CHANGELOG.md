# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- First release: Reef Drift as an Omarchy shell plugin (overlay + bar icon), a QML port of the original HTML5 game.
- Baked pixel art and audio; logic bundled from the original game and covered by ported tests.
- Known differences from the web game: the blend between night steps is an opacity crossfade rather than a dither,
  effects, music and the ocean wash are compressed separately instead of in one mix, and the wash tracks depth and night
  through three crossfaded loops.
